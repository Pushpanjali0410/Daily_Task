SHIPPING INFORMATION

Order Processing:
- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day
- You will receive an email confirmation with tracking details once your order is shipped

Delivery Timelines:
- Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2-4 business days
- Non-Metro Cities and Towns: 4-7 business days
- Remote Areas and Villages: 7-10 business days

Shipping Charges:
- Free shipping on orders above Rs. 499
- Standard shipping fee of Rs. 49 for orders below Rs. 499
- Express delivery (1-2 days) available for select pin codes at Rs. 99 extra

Tracking Your Order:
- Tracking link will be sent to your registered email and mobile number
- You can also track using your order ID through our support assistant
- Tracking updates are available every 24 hours

International Shipping:
- Currently, we only deliver within India
- International shipping is not available at this time

Delivery Issues:
- If your order has not been delivered within the expected timeline, please contact support
- In case of failed delivery attempts, our courier partner will try 3 times before returning the package
- Undelivered packages are returned to our warehouse and refunds are processed within 7-10 business days
