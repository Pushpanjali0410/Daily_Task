RETURN POLICY

Customers can return products within 7 days of delivery.

Conditions for Return:
- Product must be unused and in its original condition
- Original packaging is required
- All tags and labels must be intact
- Return request must be raised within 7 days of delivery

Non-Returnable Items:
- Perishable goods
- Personal hygiene products
- Digital downloads and software
- Customized or personalized items

Return Process:
1. Raise a return request through customer support
2. Our team will review the request within 24 hours
3. A pickup will be scheduled within 2 business days
4. Once the product is received and inspected, refund will be initiated

Refund Timeline:
- Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after the returned product is received and inspected
- Refund will be credited to the original payment method
- UPI/Wallet refunds may be faster (1-3 business days)

Exchange Policy:
- Exchanges are available for size or color variants of the same product
- Exchange requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery
